# Evidence Report: Student Resume & Job Application Friction Analysis

**Date:** October 2026  
**Target Audience:** Computer Science / Tech Students & Early-Career Job Seekers  
**Sample Size:** 16 Respondents (`evidence/Untitled form (Responses).xlsx`)  

---

## Executive Summary

Job application platforms present significant hurdles for early-career applicants. Findings from our survey confirm that tech students experience severe application fatigue, qualification uncertainty, and a complete lack of feedback when optimizing resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). 

---

## Detailed Data Breakdown

### 1. Application Strategy & Manual Effort
* **43.8% (7 respondents)** spend **15–30+ minutes manually tailoring** their resume for every single job application.
* **31.3% (5 respondents)** send an un-customized master resume to all roles due to time constraints.
* **25.0% (4 respondents)** actively refrain from applying to open positions because manual tailoring takes too much time and effort.

### 2. The Qualification Gap & Hesitation
When asked if missing 1 or 2 listed skills caused them to hesitate or refrain from applying (even when meeting core requirements):
* **37.5% (6 respondents)** answered **"Yes, frequently"** (driven by uncertainty and imposter syndrome).
* **62.5% (10 respondents)** answered **"Sometimes"**.
* **0%** reported applying without hesitation.

### 3. ATS & Optimization Awareness
* **56.3% (9 respondents)** guess and hope for the best regarding ATS screening.
* **37.5% (6 respondents)** perform painstaking line-by-line manual keyword comparisons.
* **6.3% (1 respondent)** stated they have no understanding of how ATS software evaluates candidates.

---

## Key Qualitative Pain Points

### A. Verbose & Unclear Job Descriptions
Students expressed overwhelming frustration with vague, lengthy, and bloated job posts:
* *"Job descriptions are often long, repetitive, and filled with 'nice-to-have' skills, making it hard to tell what I truly need to qualify for the role."*
* *"Too much irrelevant information and unclear job requirements—it’s hard to quickly identify key responsibilities."*
* *"It's really long and I just want to know in a summarized way what the description is about."*

### B. High Demand for Actionable Skill-Gap Roadmaps
When evaluating proposed AI capabilities, respondents overwhelmingly prioritized **exact skill gap identification** over pure automation:
* *"The most valuable feature would be personalized skill-gap analysis with a clear action plan—showing exactly what skills I’m missing for the jobs I want and what to learn next."*
* *"Something related to job suggestion according to my skills and choice, and if any gap, suggest me the roadmap to fill it."*
* *"Identifying exact skill gaps and offering targeted ways to bridge them."*

---

## Conclusion & Proposed Solution Scope

The empirical data directly validates the need for a solution that:
1. **Summarizes and extracts core requirements** from lengthy job postings.
2. **Evaluates resume match percentage accurately** without manual effort.
3. **Provides clear skill-gap identification and learning roadmaps** to turn candidate hesitation into confident applications.
